RULES

👽 THE GOAL

Humans must survive and identify the hostile players. Hostiles must eliminate enough humans to take control of the ship.

🧑‍🚀 HUMANS

Engineers, Scientists, Detectives, Medics, Captains, Guards, Survivors, Radio Operators and Judges each have different abilities.

👽 HOSTILES

Aliens, Saboteurs, Silencers and Parasites work against the crew.

🦠 INFECTION

Infection is hidden. An infected player does not know they are infected. Other players cannot see the infection normally.

The Scientist is able to detect infection.

🗳️ VOTING

At the end of each round, living players vote on who they believe should be ejected.

⚙️ SHIP SYSTEMS

Sabotage can disable ship systems. The Engineer can repair them.
